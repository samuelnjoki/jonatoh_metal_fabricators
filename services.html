01 / CUSTOM WORK

Metal Fabrication

Made-to-measure brackets, frames, stands, fittings, work tables, shelves and one-off designs. We can discuss the shape, intended load, available space and finish before work begins.

  • Custom dimensions and practical layouts
  • Material and finish options discussed before fabrication
  • Best quote details: sketch, quantity, measurements and intended use
02 / SECURITY

Gates, Doors & Grilles

Security gates, steel doors, window grilles and made-to-fit protective screens. Choose a simple, modern or decorative look and tell us how the opening will be used.

  • Sliding or hinged gate concepts
  • Decorative patterns, bars and privacy options
  • Best quote details: opening size, photos, preferred design and location
03 / STRUCTURES

Structural Steelwork

Steel frames, supports, canopies, carports, storage racks and utility structures. We can discuss site dimensions, access, roof coverage and how the structure will be used. Structural design and safety-critical specifications must be confirmed by a qualified professional where required.

  • Frames, supports and covered parking concepts
  • Planning around site access and installation conditions
  • Best quote details: site photos, dimensions, location and any drawings
04 / FINISHING

Welding & Repairs

Welding, reinforcement, alignment and repairs for suitable metal items. We first need to understand the damage, material and intended use so the repair can be assessed appropriately.

  • Repair assessment from clear photos
  • Reinforcement and finishing options
  • Best quote details: close-up photos, item size and what failed
05 / INTERIORS

Railings & Furniture

Interior and exterior handrails, balcony balustrades, stair rails, metal tables, shelves and furniture frames. We can coordinate the style with your space and discuss suitable finishes for indoor or outdoor use.

  • Contemporary, simple or decorative designs
  • Options for polished, painted or coated finishes
  • Best quote details: measurements, reference photo, quantity and setting
06 / PROJECT SUPPORT

Installation & Fitting

For projects where installation is available, fitting can be discussed together with fabrication. Site conditions matter, so we may need to understand access, fixing points, transport and whether old fittings need removal.

  • Discuss delivery and fitting as part of the quote
  • Confirm measurements and fixing points before production
  • Best quote details: site location, access photos and preferred timing
